- **In autopilot, the chief gives extra repair rounds itself, up to 5 in all (T804).** Before, a 4th round always waited for the owner's gate.
  - `round <T> --why "<reason>"` past `max_rounds` starts the next round when autopilot is on for the chief's session, up to round 5. It works from the round hold or before it, and writes a decision row with the reason. The Codex `round` tool takes `why`.
  - With autopilot off, without a reason, or for round 6 and later, the round hold and the owner's gate stay as before. A lead's `round` takes no reason.
  - A round of low fix findings only now starts, and counts like any round. So a lead also gets the next round for a low fix key that the re-check did not confirm, and no longer ends blocked for it.
