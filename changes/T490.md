- **Every review and QA finding names its run and class.** `finding add` (and the Codex `finding_add` tool) now refuses a finding whose source is a review or QA role (code-reviewer, security-reviewer, ux-reviewer, qa or evidence-review) without `--run` and `--class`, and the refusal names the classes. A known key that opens again keeps its run and class. A finding from another source, such as the sage's own note, may still leave them out. So `sage stats` can count review findings by class and by cycle from now on; older rows do not change. The sage's finding for a failed CI check names the task's lead run and the class other.
