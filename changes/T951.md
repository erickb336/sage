- **Codex: autopilot merges in Codex 0.164 and later (T951).** Before, autopilot merged only in Codex 0.164 to 0.167, so each Codex update made merges wait until a sage release. Now a newer Codex merges too, for example 0.168, 0.169 and 1.0 ([Autopilot in Codex](docs/guide/codex.md#autopilot-in-codex)).
  - An older Codex than 0.164 still refuses, and so does a version that sage cannot read.
  - sage still refuses a record of a form that it does not know, for example a new line type. Then merges wait for you, and the refusal says so.
  - The trade-off, by the owner's choice: a newer Codex can change what a record line means with no change of its form, and sage cannot see that. The merge check, the clean cycles and CI still hold.
  - The start status still warns when your Codex is outside the tested versions (0.164 to 0.167).
