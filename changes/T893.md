- **Any chief abandons a task with no owning chief, with a reason.** In a shared logbook, a chief no longer needs a claim or your takeover gate to end a task that no chief owns, such as a task that the move brought from the other provider's logbook (T893).
  - A Claude chief runs `sage task <T> set state=abandoned --why "<reason>" --chief C<n>`. A Codex chief calls `task_set` with `fields` `{"state": "abandoned"}` and `why`.
  - The task keeps no chief. The decision row `state abandoned` keeps the reason and the chief that abandoned the task.
  - An abandon with no reason refuses. A change of any other field, also together with the abandon, still needs the claim. A task of another chief still needs the takeover gate.
  - `--why` goes only with `state=abandoned`, also on a task that the chief owns.
