- **A Codex lead can now record its own task.** A child of the chief named `sage_lead_t<n>`, while its task's lead run runs, may use the sage tools for runs, findings, verdicts, rounds, pages and briefs of its own task, and move that task through a cycle. Each call on another task is refused with a line that names the rule. The rules follow the Claude lead's: a lead adds only runs of its team, dismisses or moves only a low finding, leaves a checks verdict to the chief, and repairs through a round. A reviewer or QA agent under a lead now has its report kept, so that it can back a verdict. The gate's proof of a call now carries the caller's role and task, and the sage server applies the caller's rule itself. The chief still starts no lead in Codex, so nothing changes for you yet.
