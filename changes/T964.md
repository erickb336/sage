- **A severity word in a sentence no longer makes a report unreadable (T964).** The report reader takes a finding's severity from the word after its key only. The rest of the finding line and its detail lines are text, so a low finding that says "a real medium finding", a quoted test name such as "not ok 3 a high finding is refused", or "not a blocker." no longer costs a review or QA run.
  - A line under FINDINGS still makes the report unreadable when it states a severity above that of its own finding: a severity word next to a key (`K2 high`), with marks or a colon (`(raise to medium)`, `high:`), not in lower case (`HIGH`), at the start of a line, or at the end of a line after an ordinary word (`rated high`). An indented line in the form of a finding is still never detail. A lower-case severity word after a, an or the, or between two ordinary words, is text.
  - The stop gate of each provider names the reader's problem and the proof's problem in the one stop that it gives a review or QA agent (core's `stopReason`), so that a fix of one problem cannot leave the other one unchecked. The text of that stop lives in core once.
  - The report skill and the design page give the new rule.
- **Limits.** A writer who puts a higher severity in a plain sentence under a lower finding (for example "this is a blocker for the release") gets the finding's own severity: the severity is the word after the key.
