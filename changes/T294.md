- **A moved or dismissed finding no longer needs an empty commit.** Before, a `findings` or `qa-fail` verdict refused its commit for good, also after triage moved or dismissed each of its findings. Now the merge check and the move to verified pass over such a verdict when its run links it to at least one finding, and each finding of that run is dismissed or moved. A verdict with no link, an open finding, or a finding closed as fixed still refuses, and a `checks-fail` verdict always does. A cleared verdict never counts as clean: the commit still needs its full clean cycles. The board no longer shows "QA failed" for a commit that passes the merge check (T294).
