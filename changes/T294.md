- **A moved or dismissed finding no longer needs an empty commit.** Before, a `findings` or `qa-fail` verdict refused its commit for good, also after triage moved or dismissed each of its findings. Now the merge check and the move to verified pass over such a verdict when its run links it to at least one finding, and each finding of that run is moved, or dismissed below high. A verdict with no link, an open finding, a finding closed as fixed, a dismissed high finding, or a moved finding back on the task still refuses, and a `checks-fail` verdict always does. So that a part of a bad report cannot clear it, a `findings` or `qa-fail` verdict now needs each medium and high finding of its run's report on record for that run, and a closed finding is no longer dismissed. A cleared verdict never counts as clean: the commit still needs its full clean cycles. The board no longer shows "QA failed" for a commit that passes the merge check (T294).
