- **Every principle is checked against pstack's latest text (T935).** The baseline is pstack 0.15.36, at commit 0bb6f37 of github.com/cursor/plugins.
  - Each principle's `source` line names that version and commit. Before, 12 lines named a commit that their text did not come from.
  - The [principles page](docs/guide/principles.md#baseline-against-pstack) has a baseline table: for each of the 25 principles, the pstack version checked, its status and what differs.
  - minimize-reader-load, never-block-on-the-human and subtract-before-you-add take pstack's new wording.
  - encode-lessons-in-structure takes pstack's new order of mechanisms (from pstack's `correct` skill): architecture, a type, a lint or check whose error names the fix, a test, a written rule last.
  - Smaller rules from pstack come in: attack-the-premise, explain-the-number, foundational-thinking, laziness-protocol, migrate-callers-then-delete-legacy-apis, prove-it-works and sequence-verifiable-units.
  - Four principles keep their fuller text where pstack cut its text and dropped rules: encode-lessons-in-structure, exhaust-the-design-space, guard-the-context-window and laziness-protocol. Where a rule moved to pstack's description or another pstack skill, the principle carries it.
  - migrate-callers-then-delete-legacy-apis takes pstack's conditions: it applies to a simplification or refactor that the project can take as a coordinated breaking change.
  - minimize-reader-load keeps a line for reviewers: report the load in a finding.
  - The list of all principles labels each one "from pstack" or "sage's own"; the baseline table gives its status.
