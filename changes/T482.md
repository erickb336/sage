- **A lead waits for its helpers, keeps its slot, and runs up to 9 helpers.** The sage hook now blocks a lead's stop while one of its helpers runs, and names the lead's new wait command (`node <plugin>/hooks/wait.mjs <T>`). That command needs only Bash: it returns when a helper stops, with the RESULT that the hook kept from its report. A lead's slot holds while its lead run runs, however long it waits, in the session that started it. After a restart or a crash, a new session starts a fresh lead for the task: the old session's lead slot frees after an hour with no event of that lead or its helpers, or at once when the sage ends the old lead run and records a new one. The sage's `run done` of a helper run frees that helper's slot at once. A lead runs up to 9 helpers at once (before: 3), within the project's agent cap, and a refusal at a cap names the lead's wait command. The default caps are now 10 agents for each project (`max_agents`, before: 4), so one lead and its 9 helpers fit, and 20 in all (`cap_total`, before: 12); a value in `config.json` still wins. The hook now refuses a lead's checks verdict, the sage's message to a lead that ended (start a fresh lead), and the sage's review or QA verdict on a task with a running lead run unless a finished run of that task, of the role that gives the kind, backs it with its helper's kept report.
