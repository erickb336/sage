- **Approve one more repair round.** A round hold now needs an explicit, task-specific owner approval. It permits one round above the spent rounds or current limit, whichever is greater. Adopted tasks keep their spent rounds and pull request. Reused approvals grant nothing; earlier allowances and review requirements remain. Native task creation, review acceptance, title changes and round resumes saved before an upgrade retain their saved rules, bytes and hashes. They can recover after an interruption; new resumes still require an unused approval.
