- **A milestone's word agrees with its tasks' words (T1009).** A milestone and a release now take their state word from the words of their tasks, the same words that the task cards show. Before, they used a second, shorter rule, so a milestone could say "not started" while its tasks said needs you and waiting.
  - Board page, Releases tab, chat board and `status`: an active milestone whose pilot needs you and whose other tasks wait now reads in progress, and so does its release.
  - A milestone whose only open tasks wait, also a briefed task that waits for its pilot, now reads not started, as its cards do. Before, a briefed task counted as started.
  - What stays: the six words, the task cards and the rules of T1002.
