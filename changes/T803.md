- **A lead waits for CI, and briefs a framed task itself (T803).** This takes items 6 and 8 of T668.
  - A Claude lead runs `wait <T> --ci`, and a Codex lead runs `ci-wait.mjs <T>`. Both call one core CI wait. It reads the task's PR and branch from the logbook, and waits up to 9 minutes for CI on the branch's head. It reads only `gh pr view <n> --json headRefOid` and `gh pr checks <n>`, and runs nothing else.
  - The wait prints `CI pass`, `CI fail`, `CI none` (no checks) or `CI runs` (run it again), and the next step. The lead starts a review cycle, and the re-checks of a repair, only after CI passes on the head. After `CI fail`, it ends blocked with `Left: the checks`, and the sage records `checks-fail` and the finding as before.
  - The wait does not read the checks of an older head: it waits until the PR's head is the branch's head.
  - A lead on a framed task moves it to briefed, then to building, as its first step. A framed task with a design step stays the chief's, because the chief runs the design before the brief.
  - The lead file is smaller: the rare cases of a helper with no report, or a report that the hook cannot read, moved to the `helper-gaps` reference.
