- **A finding lined up under the first one is a finding (T1008).** In a review's or QA's report, the first finding often stands on the FINDINGS line, and the next ones are indented to line up under it, as in the report skill's own layout. The report reader refused such a report, because it read an indented finding line as a line that it could not read. So a clean report backed no verdict, and the role ran again. This happened twice on 2026-11-12.
  - Now a list item with a key and a severity is a finding at any indent, also under another finding, at the severity that it gives.
  - This is the safe direction: an indented `- K2 high` under a low finding counts as high, and an indented `- K2 medium` under a high finding still counts as its own medium finding. Read as detail, either one would hide a finding.
  - A list item in the form of a finding in the detail, such as a step written as `- 1 low the first step`, is now a finding too. Before, it made the report unreadable.
  - An indented line in the form of a finding without a list mark (`K2 medium a second bug`) still makes the report unreadable. The detail under a finding still compares with that finding.
  - The report skill and the design page give the new rule.
