- **State words for tasks, milestones and releases (T1002).** sage core now maps its 15 recorded task states to the six words that you see: not started, waiting, needs you, in progress, done and stopped. Each word comes with a short reason, for example the question that you answer or the task that it waits for. Milestones and releases get a word too: a release's word comes only from the words of its items. No board or status line shows the words yet; the presentation work uses them next.
  - "Needs you" comes from the same reasons that put a card in the board's Needs you box, so the word and the box agree. The approved design changes two cases, which the board shows later: a done or stopped task keeps its word, and a merge check that refuses a pull request's head is "in progress", because the chief repairs it.
