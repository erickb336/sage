  - The review copy of the script goes with it. No task used it: reviewers read the branch, as before.
  - The hook no longer has the owner check for the script's `create` and `merge`. The push rule and the merge check keep the owner check for `git push` and `gh pr merge`.
  - The arena rules, the sage skill, the usage guide, the Codex guide and the design pages describe the plain step.
