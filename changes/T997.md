- **The state words on the board and in status (T997).** You now see the six state words of T1002 (not started, waiting, needs you, in progress, done and stopped) where a task, a milestone or a release shows its state. Each word comes from one rule in sage core, so the board, the chat and `status` agree.
  - Board page: each card shows its word with its mark (○ ⧗ ! ▶ ✓ ■) at the top right. A task page, each task row of a milestone page and each milestone shows its word too. The columns do not change.
  - Chat board ("show board"): each task line, each Needs you item and each milestone line has its word after the title, for example `T13 Sample · waiting · …`. The lookup of one task (`show board T13`) shows the word in its second line.
  - A wait names the word of the task that it waits for: "Waits for T12 (in progress)." in place of "(in review)", and "(stopped)" in place of "(abandoned)".
  - `status`: each milestone line has its word after its release, for example `milestone M5 · release 1.0 · in progress · pilot T984: 0 of 8 merged · name: Sage feel`. A done milestone reads `done · 3 of 4 merged`, and a dropped one `stopped · dropped: …`. The release line already had its word. The tasks line keeps the tool's own states.
  - A milestone whose pilot stopped, or is not in the logbook, now needs you in its release's word too, as on the board.
  - What stays: the task page's "Recorded state" line, the `task <T>` line and every write confirmation of the state tool keep the tool's own states, for the chief and the agents.
