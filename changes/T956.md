- **The gap rule in core, for Claude and Codex (T956).** A lead that leaves out a medium or high finding of a helper's report now learns it at its next step in both providers, not only at the verdict. So the Codex limit "Late check of a left-out finding" is gone from the Codex guide.
  - Core's lead policy reads the task's kept review and QA reports. While a medium or high finding of a report has no finding row of its run (`--run`) at its severity or above, the lead's next `run add` of that role, its `run done` of that run and its state move are refused. The refusal names the run and the report's keys (such as K1), and gives the `finding add` form. A low finding needs no row, and a run with no kept report holds nothing.
  - A report that cannot be read holds the same steps while its run runs, until the chief ends the run.
  - A row counts whoever recorded it, also the chief. The Claude hook no longer keeps its own notes of the findings that a lead recorded, and it no longer checks the sage's verdict while a lead runs: the state tool's verdict backing holds that verdict in every shell form.
  - The verdict backing and the gap rule share one comparison of a report's findings with the rows of its run.
