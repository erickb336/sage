- **After a move, the Codex settle closes a saved change as done (T680).** A change that the Codex chief saved before a move, but did not acknowledge in its journal, has its receipt in the old logbook. The settle step found no receipt in the new logbook and made the change void, so the old task's count of updates was one too low (this matters only when the owner restores the backup after the move).
  - The settle now also reads the receipts in the old logbooks that the new logbook's `sources.tsv` names. A change with a receipt there closes as done.
  - Only a change that was never saved is void.
  - The settle reads the old logbooks and writes neither logbook. It reads the receipts of interrupted runs from the old logbooks too.
